Entry for the [Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-notes-from-a-passive-aggressive-fridge-83c00ad7) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) writes one original funny passive-aggressive note from a smart fridge to its owner into `/output/entry.txt`, validating it against the competition gate (UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, single line, not only whitespace).

> You opened me 14 times today and took nothing. The leftovers are starting to think you're just here for the light.

Run: `python3 main.py`
